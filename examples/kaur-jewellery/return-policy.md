# Kaur Jewellery -- Return & Exchange Policy

## Returns

We want you to love your jewellery. If you're not completely satisfied,
you may return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full
refund to your original payment method.

To qualify for a return:

- The item must be unworn and in its original condition.
- The item must include all original packaging, certificates, and
  authenticity cards.
- A copy of the original receipt or order confirmation must be included.

## Non-returnable items

- Custom or engraved pieces
- Earrings, for hygiene reasons, once the packaging seal is broken
- Items purchased on final sale or clearance

## Exchanges

We're happy to exchange an item for a different size or style within 30
days of delivery, subject to the same conditions as returns. Exchanges for
a higher-priced item require payment of the difference; exchanges for a
lower-priced item are refunded the difference.

## How to start a return

Contact our support team with your order number and we'll email you a
prepaid return shipping label within one business day. Once we receive
and inspect the item, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

## International orders

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless
the item arrived damaged or defective, in which case we cover the cost.

## Damaged or defective items

If your jewellery arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of
delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We will arrange a free
replacement or full refund, your choice.
