# Kaur Jewellery -- Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping (United States)

- **Standard shipping**: 5-7 business days, $8.95 (free on orders over $150)
- **Expedited shipping**: 2-3 business days, $19.95
- **Overnight shipping**: next business day if ordered before 1pm ET, $34.95

All domestic orders are insured and require a signature on delivery for
orders over $500.

## International shipping

We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the
European Union.

- **Standard international**: 10-15 business days, $24.95
- **Expedited international**: 5-7 business days, $49.95

Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged
by their country. These are not included in the item price or shipping
cost.

## Processing time

Most items ship within 1-2 business days. Custom or engraved pieces
require an additional 5-7 business days to craft before shipping.

## Order tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.
You can also check order status anytime by logging into your account.

## Delivery issues

If a package shows as delivered but you haven't received it, please
contact us within 7 days so we can investigate with the carrier.
