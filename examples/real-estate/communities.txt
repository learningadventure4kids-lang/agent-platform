Communities We Serve

Austin, TX
Austin is known for its live music scene, tech industry growth, and no
state income tax. Popular neighborhoods include South Congress (walkable,
trendy restaurants), Mueller (planned community, family-friendly parks),
and Westlake (top-rated schools, larger lots). Average commute to downtown
from most suburbs is 20-35 minutes.

Denver, CO
Denver offers easy access to the Rocky Mountains alongside a growing
downtown core. Popular neighborhoods include LoHi (walkable, breweries and
restaurants), Washington Park (historic homes, large park), and
Highlands Ranch (family-friendly, newer construction, good schools).
Denver's light rail (RTD) connects many suburbs to downtown.

School districts
We can connect you with current school ratings and boundary maps for any
address you're considering -- ratings change year to year, so we always
pull the latest data rather than relying on older reports.

HOA considerations
Many newer communities in both Austin and Denver have a homeowners
association (HOA) with monthly or annual dues covering shared amenities
like pools, parks, and landscaping. Dues typically range from $30-$300/month
depending on the community and amenities. Always ask your agent for the
HOA's financial statements and rules before making an offer in a
HOA-governed community.

Property taxes
Texas has no state income tax but relatively high property tax rates
(roughly 1.8-2.2% of assessed value annually in the Austin area). Colorado
has comparatively lower property tax rates (roughly 0.5-0.7% in the Denver
area) but does have a state income tax.
