# ABC Realty -- Mortgage & Financing Guide

## Down payment requirements

- **Primary residence**: as low as 3% down for qualified first-time buyers
  through conventional loan programs; 5% for most other conventional loans.
- **Second home**: minimum 10% down.
- **Investment property**: minimum 20% down.

## Loan types we help buyers navigate

- **Conventional loans**: typically require a credit score of 620+.
- **FHA loans**: as low as 3.5% down with a credit score of 580+.
- **VA loans**: 0% down for eligible veterans and active-duty service members.
- **Jumbo loans**: for properties above the conforming loan limit in your
  area, usually requiring 10-20% down and strong credit.

## Pre-approval

We strongly recommend getting pre-approved before touring homes. A
pre-approval letter tells sellers you're a serious, qualified buyer and
tells you your realistic budget. Pre-approval typically takes 1-3 business
days once you provide income, asset, and credit documentation to a lender.

## Closing costs

Buyers should generally budget 2-5% of the purchase price for closing
costs, which include loan origination fees, appraisal, title insurance,
and prepaid property taxes/insurance.

## Working with our preferred lenders

While you're free to use any lender, our preferred lending partners are
familiar with local market timelines and can often close faster. Ask your
agent for an introduction.
